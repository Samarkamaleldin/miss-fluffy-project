Miss Fluffy | Digital Marketing Project

Project Overview

Miss Fluffy is a digital marketing project for an anti-dandruff shampoo product, focused on understanding the target audience, analyzing customer behavior, and developing customer-centric marketing strategies to strengthen brand communication and engagement.

Project Objectives

* Understand the target audience’s needs, preferences, and pain points.
* Analyze customer insights and buying behavior.
* Identify customer segments and develop detailed buyer personas.
* Define effective marketing messages tailored to different audience segments.
* Develop content ideas and marketing materials aligned with customer needs.
* Support brand awareness, audience engagement, and purchase decisions.

Project Scope

* Target Audience Research
* Customer Insights Analysis
* Audience Segmentation
* Buyer Persona Development
* Marketing Strategy
* Content Strategy & Planning
* Marketing Communication
* Creative Marketing Materials
